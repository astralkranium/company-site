Build it your way

  • 260+ skills to choose from
  • 17 skill trees
  • 1 in 3 of an Eternal's skills fit in one campaign
  • 15 ways to make up your party

A campaign gives you about 28 skill points. Learning everything one Eternal has costs more than 80, so every point is a choice.

  • Choices that stick

    Some skills lock each other out, and you get one respec per playthrough. Commit to your build.

  • Skills earned in battle

    Five skills unlock through deeds in a fight. Synn only learns Possess after 15 mind controls.

  • Heroes that work together

    Synn's Drenched makes Frazzah's Frozen harder to break. Frazzah's Chilled makes Morrathil's Rooted harder to escape.

  • Set up, then strike

    Frazzah's Thrust pierces deeper into any stunned enemy. Roki's Sandworn makes a target take more damage from every source.

  • Look after your allies

    Psychic Haste gives a friend extra actions and movement. Cleansing Rain cures Bleeding, Poisoned and Burned.

  • Eternal relics

    Each Eternal earns a Legacy Relic that reshapes their signature skills. Later, you forge it into an Epic one.

Four Eternals

  • Ara the Geomancer

    Fights with pebbles: ricochet shots at range, a pebble shield up close, and Roki, a sentient pebble that grows with her.

    Skill trees Stoneward Companion · Pebble Volley · Gravitic Control · Geode Cartographer

  • Frazzah the Cryomancer Knight

    Build him as a pure swordmaster, or as a cryomancer who fills the field with up to seven ice clones.

    Skill trees Swordmaster's Legacy · Frostforged Mastery · Glacier Echoes · Hearthwarden

  • Morrathil the Wrath of the Forest

    Spreads plague and blight, and raises an undead treant that evolves in stages beside him.

    Skill trees Revenant Gardens · Hive Confluence · Plaguebringer · Blightwright Tactics

  • Synn the Leviathan's Daughter

    Moves through stealth and mist, turns enemies against their own side, and can possess them outright.

    Skill trees Psychovore Elegy · Breathless Veil · Tidal Aegis · Puppeteer's Mandate

Each Eternal has four skill trees of their own, and all four share a fifth, Temporal Weave, built around Shimmer.

Gear, ground and monsters

  • 66 handcrafted items

    Each has a fixed effect. Reset every cooldown with the Chronosphere, or rise again with the Crown of the Undying.

  • The Blacksmith

    Epics never drop. You forge all 16, including a Cinderscale Egg that hatches a Black Dragon ally.

  • 11 terrain types

    Lava burns, snow chills you until you freeze, and some skills reshape the map mid-fight.

  • 20 conditions

    Stunned, Horrified, Burned, Poisoned, Hasted and more. Drenched puts out Burned, and Chilled stacks into Frozen.

  • Monsters with tricks

    Over 20 kinds of monster that flank, focus fire and stalk from stealth, led by demon commanders Vexaroth and Baron Valdris.

  • Elites

    Elites roll random affixes from a pool of 17, like Impervious, Regenerative, Venomous and Fireborn.

On the battlefield

  • Shimmer

    Gather Shimmer from the world and fallen monsters. When a die misses, spend it and the die lands on its best result.

  • Move, then act

    Moving never costs an action. Engagement and attacks of opportunity reward careful positioning.

  • Stealth

    Contested dice rolls decide who gets seen. Strike from stealth for extra true damage.

  • Sight and fog of war

    Creatures and walls block shots, and you only see what your team sees.

Ways to play

  • An eight-chapter campaign

    About 6 to 8 hours, plus a tutorial. Each chapter has its own objective, from clearing the field to getting villagers to safety.

  • Online co-op

    Up to 4 players over Steam. Monsters scale with your party, and a fallen Eternal gets back up after two rounds.

  • Local co-op

    2 players on one PC, through the whole campaign. Steam Remote Play Together works too.

  • PvP

    Capture the Box in 1v1 or 2v2, or Judgement Hill, a deathmatch for up to four sides, free-for-all or in teams.

  • Controller support

    The whole game works on a pad, with Xbox or PlayStation button prompts.

  • The little things

    Mid-level checkpoints, Steam Cloud saves, 23 achievements, a damage breakdown for every hit, and one key to repeat your last ability.

How it started

Eternally started three years ago as a question: what would a PvP RPG look like if it played like a board game, for 2 to 4 players? Then the playtesters kept teaming up. So co-op grew into a full campaign, and PvP stayed in as its own mode.

I've put my heart and soul into this game. I hope you enjoy playing it as much as I've enjoyed making it.

Adam

Short description

Eternally is a turn-based dark fantasy RPG on a hex grid, where every attack is a dice roll. Build one of four Eternals from over 260 skills across 17 skill trees. One campaign holds about a third of an Eternal's skills, so every run plays differently. Play alone, with up to three friends online, or with one friend on the same PC.

AI content

Three non-playable characters have AI-generated voices: the Celestial, who narrates the story, and the villains Vexaroth and Valdris. A small number of sound effects were also made with AI tools. The playable Eternals are voiced by human actors. Nothing in the game is generated by AI while you play.
