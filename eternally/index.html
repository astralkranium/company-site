The game

Eternally is a turn-based dark fantasy RPG on a hex grid, for Windows on Steam. Every attack is a dice roll, so every turn carries a little risk.

You play as one of four Eternals. Where you stand on the grid decides what you can reach and who can reach you, and sometimes the dice just say no.

Build from over 250 skills and a set of relics made for your Eternal, then take them through an eight-level campaign. Play it alone, with up to three friends online, or with one friend on the same PC.

How it started

Eternally started three years ago as a question: what would a PvP RPG look like if it played like a board game? A hex grid, dice that decide every swing, and 2 to 4 players trying to outthink each other.

Then the playtests surprised me. Players kept teaming up, and the co-op moments were the ones they talked about afterwards. So co-op grew into a full campaign, with PvP still at its core.

I've put my heart and soul into this game for three years. I hope you enjoy playing it as much as I've enjoyed making it.

Adam

Shimmer

You gather Shimmer on the battlefield and from fallen enemies. When a die misses, spend Shimmer and that die turns into its highest roll.

The Temporal Weave skill tree builds on it, with more ways to bend a turn your way. Some items push it further still.

Four Eternals

  • Ara the Geomancer

    Fights with pebbles: ricochet shots at range, or a pebble shield up close.

  • Frazzah the Cryomancer Knight

    Build him as a pure swordmaster, or as a cryomancer who leaves an ice clone behind for every enemy he kills.

  • Morrathil the Wrath of the Forest

    Spreads plague and blight, and fights beside a Treant familiar.

  • Synn the Leviathan's Daughter

    Moves through stealth and mist, and can mind control and possess her enemies.

Each Eternal has their own skill trees and their own relics.

What else is in it

  • Dice on a hex grid

    Every attack is a dice roll. Elite enemies roll affixes such as Impervious, Regenerative and Ruthless, so a fight you think you know can still go sideways.

  • Eight campaign levels

    About 6 to 8 hours. Some levels ask you to defeat every enemy on the field. Others are longer quests, such as getting villagers to safety before it is too late.

  • Co-op and PvP

    Online co-op for up to 4 players. Local co-op for 2 players on one PC, each with their own Eternal. PvP in 1v1, 2v2 and Deathmatch. Steam Remote Play Together works too.

  • Builds

    Over 250 skills across the four Eternals, and relics you can upgrade into epic relics as the campaign goes on. You get one full skill respec per playthrough.

  • Controller support

    The whole game works on a pad, from combat to menus and the lobby. Xbox and PlayStation controllers both work, and the button prompts match the one you're holding.

  • The little things

    Mid-level checkpoints, so a lost fight doesn't cost you the whole level. Steam Cloud saves, Steam achievements, and a damage breakdown that shows where every hit came from.

AI content

Three non-playable characters have AI-generated voices: the Celestial, who narrates the story, and the villains Vexaroth and Valdris. A small number of sound effects were also made with AI tools. The playable Eternals are voiced by human actors. Nothing in the game is generated by AI while you play.
