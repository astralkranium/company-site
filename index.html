Eternally

Save your timeline.

A dark fantasy tactical RPG with dice combat on a hex grid. Pick one of four Eternals, build from over 250 skills, and fight through an eight-level campaign alone, with up to three friends online, or with one friend on the same PC.

When a die misses, spend Shimmer and it turns into its highest roll.
